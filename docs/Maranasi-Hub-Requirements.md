# Maranasi Hub — Requirements & Discovery Document

**Purpose.** This document is how we capture *exactly* how Maranasi runs events today, so the Hub is built around your real workflow — not a generic template. Please answer what you can in writing; anything unclear we'll resolve in a working session. Wherever you already have it, attach **real examples** (a past proposal, a contract, a production checklist, an invoice, a vendor list).

**How to read it.** Each section has **Questions for Maranasi** (what we need from you) and, where useful, a **Starter** (our current assumption — confirm, edit, or replace it). Sections 7 and 8 are the most important: they capture how **weddings, corporate events, and private events differ**, and the **exceptions** that break the "normal" flow.

**Questions for Maranasi**

- For each role: what should they be able to **see** (all events? only theirs?) and **do** (create proposals, edit budgets, message vendors, see financials)?
- How many people on the team today? Expected in 12 months?
- Who is the single "owner" of an event end-to-end, and who supports?

**Starter (confirm/adjust):** Owner, Producer, Coordinator, Assistant — Owner sees everything; Producer owns assigned events; Coordinator/Assistant support; financial figures visible to Owner + Producer only.

## 7. Event Types — Weddings vs Corporate vs Private (and their exceptions)

This is the section that makes or breaks the fit. Each type has a different rhythm, different decision-makers, and different "gotchas." Please fill in per type.

### 7.1 Weddings & engagements
- Do you handle **multi-event** journeys (engagement / khutbe → katb al-kitab → henna night → wedding)? Should these link as one client "journey" or separate events?
- Who are the **decision-makers**? (Couple, but often parents/mothers — does the portal need to be shared with family?)
- **Cultural/religious elements** to support: zaffe (procession), dabke/sahja, live oud/tarab, religious officiant and **katb al-kitab** scheduling, modest/conservative options.
- **Seating**: mixed vs **gender-segregated** sections? Women-only areas? Does this affect floor plans and staffing?
- **Timing constraints**: prayer times, sunset for ceremony/photos, **Ramadan** blackout or special handling, summer heat (outdoor/Dead Sea).
- **Guest dynamics**: large counts (250–600+), late RSVPs, "+1 by family obligation," children, VIP family guests.
- **Exceptions to capture:** weather for outdoor venues, family disputes/last-minute scope changes, postponement (deposit/date rules), guest count surging past contract, dietary/halal requirements, dress/abaya provisions.

### 7.2 Corporate & conferences
- Who's the buyer? (Marketing, HR, exec office, a **procurement** process with a PO?)
- **Multiple approvers / sign-off chain** — how many stakeholders, and does the proposal route through approvals?
- **Branding**: client brand guidelines, sponsor logos, signage (bilingual?), step-and-repeat.
- **Program**: agenda, speakers, run-of-show, AV/stage, livestream/recording, simultaneous translation.
- **Registration**: guest lists, badges, check-in, NDAs.
- **VIP / protocol**: government or **royal attendees** (common in Jordan) — security, seating protocol, motorcade, press handling.
- **Invoicing** to a company entity, PO numbers, VAT, net-30 payment terms.
- **Exceptions to capture:** speaker cancellations, attendance far above/below forecast, security clearance changes, press embargoes, last-minute exec demands.

### 7.3 Private events (galas, anniversaries, birthdays, milestone parties)
- How do these differ from weddings in scope and decision-making?
- Privacy/discretion requirements (no public photos, NDA for staff/vendors)?
- **Exceptions to capture:** surprise events (comms must stay hidden from the guest of honor), shifting headcounts, theme changes.

### 7.4 Cross-cutting — for every type
- **What breaks the standard milestone template?** List the real exceptions you hit.
- **Date / venue change** mid-production — how should the system re-time everything?
- **Budget change** after booking — upsells, scope cuts, overruns: how tracked and re-approved?
- **Partial scope** (client brings their own photographer, say) — how do you price and plan around it?
- **Force majeure** (weather, political events, COVID-type disruptions) — your policy.
